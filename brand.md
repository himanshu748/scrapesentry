# Brand: ScrapeSentry

_Generated by the `brand-design` skill on 2026-08-21._

## Palette: Mineral Paper

**Vibe:** technical, premium and durable
**Category:** infrastructure and data
**Mood:** technical and premium
**References:** Factory, Apple and Warp

Mineral Paper keeps the existing ScrapeSentry identity: warm technical paper, near-black graphite, restrained green for verified output and red only for quarantine or failure. Mineral teal is the interaction accent. It must not replace the verified-green semantic.

## Typography: Instrument Serif + Inter + JetBrains Mono

- **Display:** Instrument Serif
- **Body and product UI:** Inter
- **Evidence, metrics and code:** JetBrains Mono

The fonts are wired through `next/font/google` in `dashboard/app/layout.tsx`. CSS variables `--font-display`, `--font-sans` and `--font-mono` are available throughout the app.

Use Instrument Serif only for primary landing headlines and major narrative section headings. Dashboard headings, controls, tables and all operator surfaces stay in Inter. Use JetBrains Mono for collector IDs, contract fields, metrics, receipts and terminal output.

## Gradients: not used

Gradients are intentionally excluded from the brand system. The technical and premium direction depends on paper surfaces, physical borders, exact typography and semantic color. Product-specific scanning beams and data routes may use functional gradients when they communicate state or motion.

## Tone and voice

### Words to use

Use direct, specific and number-forward language. Prefer verbs over adjectives. State what was detected, blocked, preserved or verified. Use measured confidence and name the evidence behind a claim.

### Words to avoid

Avoid hype such as revolutionary, game-changing, unleash, supercharge or seamless. Do not say a recovery is complete when only recorded evidence exists. Avoid manufactured urgency, exclamation marks and vague claims such as production ready without the supporting configuration.

### Voice example

> Contract score fell to 64. Twelve unsafe rows were quarantined. The last verified snapshot remains available while recovery waits for approval.

## Usage rules

**Do:**

- Use Mineral Paper for major page surfaces and graphite for primary text.
- Use mineral teal for links, focus rings and interaction accents.
- Use green only for verified, healthy or released states.
- Use red only for quarantined, destructive or failed states.
- Keep product imagery and contract-gate diagrams as the primary visual evidence.
- Use square or low-radius geometry for technical surfaces.
- Use tabular mono numerals for values that update in place.
- Test product work at 360, 768, 1024 and 1440 px.

**Do not:**

- Recolor verified states with mineral teal.
- Add decorative gradients, glass panels or floating color blobs.
- Apply Instrument Serif to the operator console, tables or buttons.
- Hide incomplete production configuration behind optimistic copy.
- Hardcode a new color when an existing semantic token fits.
- Use `transition: all`.
